Architect's Statement: The Poetics of Home

A house is the most intimate piece of architecture any human being inhabits. Long before architecture becomes a civic monument, it is the angle of morning light across a dining table, the acoustic calm of a thick brick wall, and the sheltered threshold of a front porch during rain.

My passion for architecture started with a pocket tape measure and graph-paper notebooks—sketching floor plans to understand how moving a single window or carving out a small courtyard could transform a dark room. In high school, that curiosity evolved into building physical basswood models, learning Rhino and AutoCAD, and studying passive solar principles so homes work with local climate rather than against it.

In university, I look forward to rigorous design studios, structural mechanics, and hands-on design-build workshops where I can learn to craft durable, humane homes for evolving communities.
