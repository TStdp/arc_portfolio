Why I Want to Design Homes

A house is the most intimate piece of architecture any person ever inhabits. Before architecture is a skyline or a monument, it is the warmth of morning sunlight hitting a kitchen table, the shelter of a deep roof overhang during rain, and the quiet threshold between a public street and a family living room.

Growing up, I loved measuring rooms with a pocket tape measure and rearranging floor plans on graph paper to see how a single window or courtyard could transform a dark room. That curiosity grew into building physical basswood scale models, learning CAD and Rhino, and studying passive solar principles so homes stay comfortable with minimal energy.

In university, I look forward to rigorous design studios, structural engineering courses, and hands-on fabrication workshops where I can learn how great residential architecture serves communities sustainably and humanely.
